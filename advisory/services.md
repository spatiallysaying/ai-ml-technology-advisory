# Advisory Services

- Review solution architecture, system boundaries and key technical choices.
- Evaluate models, platforms and implementation options against requirements.
- Assess feasibility, risks and paths from prototype to production.
- Advise on GenAI, retrieval-augmented generation and intelligent automation.

## GeoAI and Geospatial Systems

- Advise on computer vision for satellite, aerial and remote-sensing imagery.
- Review geospatial machine-learning and feature-extraction approaches.
- Assess GIS/AI integration, spatial analytics and large-scale processing.
- Help teams evaluate data, model and deployment trade-offs for GeoAI systems.

## Technical Strategy and Reviews

- Provide architecture and design reviews with prioritized recommendations.
- Support technical due diligence and feasibility assessments.
- Review technical sections of RFP/RFI responses and solution proposals.
- Help define evaluation criteria, risks, milestones and next steps.

Services are advisory-first. Any implementation work, deliverables and access to
systems or data must be explicitly scoped and agreed in writing.
