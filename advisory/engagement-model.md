# Engagement Models

## Monthly Advisory Retainer

A defined monthly allocation for recurring architecture reviews, advisory
sessions, technology evaluation and review of proposals or prototypes. Scope,
availability, response expectations and unused-hour treatment are agreed in
advance.

## Scoped Technical Assignment

A time-bounded assignment for a specific question or decision, such as an
architecture assessment, feasibility review, model/platform evaluation or
technical roadmap. The scope, deliverables, schedule and fee are agreed before
work begins.

## RFP/RFI or Proposal Review

A focused review of technical requirements, architecture, assumptions, risks and
response completeness. The client remains responsible for business commitments,
pricing and final submission.

## Starting an Engagement

1. Begin with a high-level discussion that avoids sharing confidential details.
2. Confirm fit, availability and any applicable conflict or outside-activity
   requirements.
3. Agree in writing on scope, deliverables, schedule, fees and confidentiality
   terms before work begins.

No confidential, proprietary or restricted information belonging to another
organization is requested, used or disclosed. Engagements proceed only where
applicable obligations permit.
